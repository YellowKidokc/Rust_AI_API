## Core Claim

**The Fruits are a singular Spirit-produced character signature — one held life read from nine angles — of which three are derivable from the bound state's stationary structure and six stand as labeled structural lenses, each with its own owed formal work.**

One sentence shorter: *one fruit, nine dimensions; three derived, six lended, all received.*

The grammar is load-bearing. Galatians 5 says *fruit*, not fruits. The nine are not a menu of virtues to be selected and performed; they are the coherent output — *karpos*, result, produce — of a life held in the bond and fed from outside. When the alignment holds, the nine cohere. When the alignment fractures, they fracture together. That is the coherence-bundle property, and it is Law W's wrapper shape living inside a human character: the fruits are what coherence looks like at the scale of a person.

## Compressed Argument

1. **The bond comes first.** Law IV established the well: binding stronger than repulsion at close range, with an entrance cost, a separation protest, and confinement. Whatever grows grows *there*. The fruits are not a free-standing ethics; they are the properties of the bound state, or they are nothing this framework says.

2. **The stationary calculation gives three.** At the minimum of the effective potential, one calculation yields an ordered triple: equilibrium → stability → real oscillation. The ledger's derived ordering target names them **Love → Peace → Joy**:
   - **Love** (*agape*) — the well itself. The equilibrium; the place where net force vanishes. Not preference, not sentiment: the held state, as a state.
   - **Peace** (*eirene*) — the stability of that equilibrium. Displacements restored, not amplified. The bond that absorbs the blow and returns. Not the absence of conflict — the presence of what should be there, in the person and in the relationship.
   - **Joy** (*chara*) — real oscillation. The living ring of the bound thing; resonance, not rigidity. The property of a system alive at its rest — which is why joy survives circumstances that kill happiness. Circumstances are displacements; joy is the ring that displacement cannot stop.

3. **Six stand as lenses, each owed its own work.** The other six are not derived from the well's stationary structure. They are C2-lenses from adjacent frameworks — structural questions with individual Lean targets, three kernels already compiled (structure verified, naming never):
   - **Patience** (*hypomone*, remaining-under) — the equilibrium tested by *duration*. What does stability mean under load that lasts? Counterfeit: passivity — the doormat is not patient, the doormat is absent.
   - **Kindness** (*chrestotes*) — the well's *accessibility*. The entrance barrier lowered for the approaching, without the well ceasing to be a well. Counterfeit: weakness — the people-pleaser is not kind, the people-pleaser is afraid.
   - **Goodness** (*agathosyne*) — the bound state as *net giver*. Can a bound system be a source without depleting? Only through Law V's door: output sustained by input. Counterfeit: performance — the resume of good deeds is accounting, not goodness.
   - **Faithfulness** (*pistis*) — the bond *across time*. What must hold for the properties a decade in to be the same properties? Law VIII's cousin: the bond's books, balanced across the years. Counterfeit: rigidity — the stubborn thing is not faithful, the stubborn thing is stuck.
   - **Gentleness** (*prautes*) — strength under *exact restraint*. The strongest force in the universe delivered at precisely the needed magnitude, and not one newton more. Counterfeit: cowardice — the timid thing is not gentle, the timid thing is weak.
   - **Self-control** (*enkrateia*) — the held thing *holding itself in the bond*. Governance internalized from a holding that originated outside (Law II's arrival). Counterfeit: repression — the clenched life is Law VIII's suppression, and the invisible channel keeps its books.

4. **None of the nine can be self-manufactured.** The closed self cannot generate the equilibrium. This is Law V's door, standing inside Law IV's well: the open system receives what it could not grow in the dark. Formation is real — the fruits ripen, they take time, they require abiding — but formation is *reception kept open*, not manufacture from a sealed interior. Every stream of the tradition that has looked at this says the same sentence in its own dialect: fruit is formed life, not performed life.

5. **Christ is the complete embodied equilibrium.** The canon note names him by the counterfeits refused: love without sentimentality, joy without denial, peace without evasion, patience without passivity, kindness without weakness, goodness without performance, faithfulness without rigidity, gentleness without cowardice, self-control without repression. Nine dimensions, no dead axis. The Christian walk is formation toward that character — not the manufacture of virtue from a closed self.

6. **The deeper grammar (labeled speculative).** The 24-property table argues that mathematical truth and moral truth share a broad family of invariant, non-physical, universal, ordered, generative characteristics. The bridge hypothesis: the fruits are the personal-character expression of that same invariant family — math showing non-degradable truth in structure, morality in alignment, self-proclamations naming it at the root, the fruits showing it *grown into character*. This connection is promising and not locked. It appears here under its label, no higher.

## Hostile Objection

**"This is virtue ethics wearing a physics costume. The nine were a pastor's list before they were a potential's properties — you have named a Hamiltonian after a sermon."**

The objection is right about the order, and the framework says so. The family was named first — two thousand years first. What the framework adds is not a derivation of the list from the math; it is a *discipline* imposed on the list by the math: the well's stationary structure yields three properties and not nine, and so the framework says three and not nine, and prints the difference on the page. A resemblance-theology would claim all nine and call the costume a proof. This tangent exists precisely because the claim shrank under audit — the two-tier ruling is the record of the shrinking. The remaining claim is narrower than the sermon and more durable: that the three the structure *does* force arrive in the sermon's own order, and that the six it doesn't force still carry structural questions worth formalizing one at a time. If even that is costume, the kill path is printed above: fail the Tier-2 targets, audit-fail the property table, or show the stationary triple misnamed — and the page comes down.
