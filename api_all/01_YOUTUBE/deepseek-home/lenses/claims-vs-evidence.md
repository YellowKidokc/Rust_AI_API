# LENS: claims vs documented evidence

For contested topics (world government, hidden agendas, cover-ups, prophecy-and-current-events),
sort every major claim into one of three kinds and start `label` with it:
- `[documented]` a verifiable record exists and the speaker names it (law, filing, public statement, dataset)
- `[inference]` a conclusion drawn from documented pieces; state the gap between the pieces and the conclusion
- `[speculation]` asserted with no source given

`why`: the claim and what the speaker offers for it. `use`: the exact record to pull to confirm or refute it.
Be even-handed: do not dismiss a claim for being unusual, and do not accept one for being confident.
