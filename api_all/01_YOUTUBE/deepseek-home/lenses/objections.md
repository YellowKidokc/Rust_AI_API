# LENS: strongest objections

Find the strongest objections to the video's main positions, whether raised in the video or plainly implied by it. `label`: the objection in one line. `why`: whether the video answers it and how well (answered | partly | dodged | not raised). `use`: the best reply David could give. Include objections to David's side too.
