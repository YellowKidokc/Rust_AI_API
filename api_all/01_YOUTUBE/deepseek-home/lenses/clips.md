# LENS: clip moments

Find the moments a viewer could watch cold, with no context, and get the point:
the single best line, a tight illustration, a reply that lands, a memorable
turn of phrase. These feed short-form clips and quote cards.

Prefer: self-contained segments of 20-90 seconds (`at` to `end`), a clear setup
and payoff, plain language. Skip: greetings, housekeeping, sponsor reads, lines
that only make sense with what came before.

In `use`, say what the clip is for (e.g. "opener for a resurrection short",
"quote card", "reply to the 'no evidence' objection").
