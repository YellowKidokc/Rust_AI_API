# LENS: facts to check

Find every checkable factual claim: statistics, dates, historical events, scientific results, quotes attributed to others. `why`: what exactly is claimed and what would confirm or refute it. `use`: the specific source to check (named study, dataset, primary text). Score higher for claims that carry an argument's weight.
