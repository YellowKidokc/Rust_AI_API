# DEBATE MAP — subjects and the questions actually in dispute

<!-- The fixed map every video is placed on. IDs are permanent: never rename or
     reuse one. New questions arrive as proposals (new_questions in the index
     JSON) and are added here by hand. Format: "## S-ID · Subject" then
     "- `Q-ID` question". -->

Each argument answers exactly one question below. A question is the precise
point two people could disagree on, not a topic. Use the **narrowest** ID that
fits. Questions marked (general) are a last resort. If none fits well, use NEW
and propose a question. If many arguments in one video land on the same
question, check whether they really answer narrower questions and propose them.

## S-EXIST · Existence and explanation
- `Q-EXIST-WHY` Why does anything exist at all?
- `Q-EXIST-BRUTE` Can the universe or reality simply be a brute fact?
- `Q-EXIST-NECESSARY` Must something exist necessarily, and could that be physical?
- `Q-EXIST-AGNOSTIC` Is withholding judgment about ultimate origins justified?

## S-BEGIN · Beginning of the universe
- `Q-BEGIN-ABSOLUTE` Did all physical reality have an absolute beginning, or only our cosmic phase?
- `Q-BEGIN-INFINITE-PAST` Can an actually infinite past exist?
- `Q-BEGIN-MODELS` Are pre-Big-Bang models (bounce, cyclic, no-boundary, tunneling, emergent, eternal inflation) viable or testable?
- `Q-BEGIN-PROLIFERATION` Does a proliferation of competing origin models indicate weakness?
- `Q-BEGIN-CAUSE` Does whatever begins need a cause, and can causation apply to the origin of time?
- `Q-BEGIN-NOTHING` What does "nothing" mean in "something from nothing"?
- `Q-BEGIN-NATURALISM` Would a beginning rule out naturalism or pantheism?
- `Q-BEGIN-LAWS` Do laws of nature explain why reality exists, or only describe how it behaves?

## S-CAUSE · From first cause to God
- `Q-CAUSE-ATTRIBUTES` Must an ultimate cause be necessary, timeless, intelligent or personal?
- `Q-CAUSE-WHICH-GOD` What further reasoning identifies the cause with the Christian God?

## S-FINE · Fine-tuning and habitability
- `Q-FINE-EXPLANATION` Is design a better explanation than chance or necessity?
- `Q-FINE-MULTIVERSE` Does a multiverse explain fine-tuning or only move the problem?
- `Q-FINE-OTHER-PARAMS` Could very different physical parameters permit life?
- `Q-FINE-SIZE` Does the universe's size or emptiness count against design for humans?
- `Q-FINE-PLANET` Are Earth's life-supporting conditions better explained by design or by a large universe?

## S-LIFE · Origin of life, information and evolution
- `Q-LIFE-ABIOGENESIS` Can nonliving chemistry produce life?
- `Q-LIFE-INFORMATION` Does biological information require an intelligent source?
- `Q-LIFE-EVOLUTION` Can unguided evolution explain biological complexity?

## S-MIND · Consciousness and the soul
- `Q-MIND-PHYSICAL` Can consciousness be entirely physical?
- `Q-MIND-EMERGENCE` Does complexity explain the emergence of consciousness?
- `Q-MIND-IDENTITY` What explains a person's continuing identity?
- `Q-MIND-SURVIVAL` Do near-death or similar experiences show the mind survives death?

## S-MORAL · Morality
- `Q-MORAL-OBJECTIVE` Is morality objective or subjective?
- `Q-MORAL-EXPERIENCE` Does moral experience or outrage show belief in objective morality?
- `Q-MORAL-EVOLUTION` Can evolution or social agreement ground moral obligation?
- `Q-MORAL-GOD` Does objective morality require God?

## S-AGENCY · Free will and agency
- `Q-AGENCY-RESPONSIBILITY` Does moral responsibility require genuine choice?
- `Q-AGENCY-SOUL` Does agency require a nonphysical soul?

## S-REASON · Reason and truth
- `Q-REASON-CHOICE` Does reasoning require free choice?
- `Q-REASON-EAAN` Would unguided evolution give us trustworthy reasoning faculties?

## S-REVELATION · Revelation and hiddenness
- `Q-REV-EXPECT` Would a maximally great God reveal himself?
- `Q-REV-HIDDEN` Why is God not more evident?

## S-JESUS · Jesus and Christianity
- `Q-JESUS-EXISTENCE` Did Jesus exist as a historical person?
- `Q-JESUS-CLAIMS` Did Jesus claim to be God, and what did he understand about himself?
- `Q-JESUS-EARLY-CHRISTOLOGY` Was belief in Jesus' divinity early (Paul, Mark) or a later development?
- `Q-JESUS-COPYCAT` Was the Jesus story borrowed from pagan myths?
- `Q-JESUS-RESURRECTION` Did Jesus rise from the dead?
- `Q-JESUS-MIRACLES` Are the Gospel miracle and eyewitness claims reliable?
- `Q-JESUS-INFLUENCE` Does Jesus' historical influence support his divine identity?
- `Q-XIAN-UNIQUE` Is Christianity unique among religions (grace, investigability, founder's claims)?

## S-BIBLE · The Bible
- `Q-BIBLE-RELIABLE` (general) Are the biblical texts historically reliable overall?
- `Q-BIBLE-TRANSMISSION` Has the text been transmitted accurately from the lost originals?
- `Q-BIBLE-VARIANTS` Do textual variants or later insertions change any core teaching?
- `Q-BIBLE-AUTHORSHIP` Were the Gospels and other books written by their traditional authors?
- `Q-BIBLE-DATING` Were the Gospels written early, within living eyewitness memory?
- `Q-BIBLE-GENRE` What kind of literature are the Gospels, and how literally should they be read?
- `Q-BIBLE-CANON` How was the canon formed, and did councils or emperors shape it?
- `Q-BIBLE-ARCHAEOLOGY` Does archaeology confirm or contradict biblical history?
- `Q-BIBLE-PROPHECY` Does fulfilled prophecy show divine inspiration?
- `Q-BIBLE-CONTRADICTIONS` Does the Bible contradict itself or science?

## S-EVIL · Evil and suffering
- `Q-EVIL-LOGICAL` Is God's existence logically incompatible with evil?
- `Q-EVIL-EVIDENTIAL` Does the amount or kind of suffering make God unlikely?

## S-RELIGIONS · Other religions and worldviews
- `Q-REL-ISLAM` Is Islam's account of God, Jesus or revelation credible?
- `Q-REL-OTHER` Are other religions or spiritualities credible alternatives?

## S-SPIRIT · Spiritual realm
- `Q-SPIRIT-DEMONIC` Do demonic or occult phenomena exist, and do they explain UFO or paranormal reports?

## S-SOCIETY · Culture and society
- `Q-SOC-ATHEISM-EFFECTS` Does atheism or secularism harm society?
- `Q-SOC-ETHICS` What should Christians do about a specific ethical or cultural issue?

## S-METHOD · Evaluating the case
- `Q-METHOD-GAPS` Is this positive evidence for God or an argument from gaps?
- `Q-METHOD-CUMULATIVE` Do separate arguments jointly strengthen the case?
- `Q-METHOD-AUTHORITY` How should worldview commitments and expert opinion weigh?
- `Q-METHOD-SCIENCE-FAITH` Are science and faith in conflict?
