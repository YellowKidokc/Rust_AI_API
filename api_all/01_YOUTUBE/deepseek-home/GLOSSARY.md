- **God Is**: the single admitted root axiom of the framework. Admitted, not proven.
- **Supporting node**: a definition or derived result that hangs off the root. Not an additional axiom.
- **Local assumption**: a premise used in one argument and labeled as such. Never a root axiom.
- **Mapping**: a link from a claim to the root or a supporting node. Not a proof. Label it speculative, defensible, or Lean-checked.
- **Lean 4**: the proof assistant used to machine-check the framework's theorems.
- **Pill**: the smallest record in the proof registry; one item per pill.
- **Isomorphism**: a structure-preserving correspondence between a physical law and a theological counterpart. Proven only when the correspondence itself is checked in Lean 4; otherwise call it a *candidate isomorphism*.
- **Master equation**: not yet defined here. Do not cite or map to it.
- **Coherence**: not yet defined here. Do not use as a technical term.
- **χ (chi)**: not yet defined here. Do not use.
