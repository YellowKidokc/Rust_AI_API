QUESTION 1 — ARGUE AGAINST YOURSELF  

Theory T is wrong. Here is the strongest case I can build in roughly 600 words (≈ 3 450 characters).

1. Selection-bias shell game  
The “70 domain survey” was compiled after the hypothesis existed. The curators cherry-picked sub-disciplines that could be shoe-horned into the coherence/decoherence vocabulary and quietly ignored dozens that do not cooperate. Where is plate tectonics? Where is paleography, actuarial science, combinatorial game theory, heterogeneous catalysis, phonology, or algebraic topology? These fields were either ignored or dissolved into already-friendly categories (e.g. “materials science,” “linguistics”) precisely to dodge falsification. A proper adversarial sample—generated by random number on the full MSC/NAICS/SIC classification trees—would certainly surface areas where the ten-law template looks embarrassingly forced.

2. Confirmation-bias framing  
Every mapping was produced by believers, not critics. Start instead with a double-blind design: give independent coders domain literature with the framework labels removed and ask them to discover whether ten natural clusters arise. They will not. Without the over-prompting (“find attraction/entropy/observer etc.”) the literature will cluster along domain-specific axes—symmetry breaking, renormalisation, niche adaptation, etc.—and the ten “axioms” will never surface. The current convergence therefore proves nothing more than that humans (and language models) can be primed to over-pattern-match.

3. Vocabulary dilutes falsifiability  
“Coherence,” “entropy,” “grace,” “faith,” “signal,” “noise” … these words are so elastic that almost any dichotomy can be re-labelled to fit. In law, “precedent” becomes coherence; in criminology, “broken-windows disorder” becomes decoherence; in structural engineering, “buckling” becomes decoherence. The framework never states operational definitions tight enough to exclude an example. That is the hallmark of an unfalsifiable theory. Compare with thermodynamics: Boltzmann entropy excludes a crystal at 0 K from being “high-entropy.” The T-framework cannot produce an analogous exclusion because the terms are rhetorical, not quantitative.

4. Formal fallacy of shared algebraic form  
The Sharpe ratio, the SNR formula, and the “faith-to-distortion” ratio all reduce to “useful quantity divided by unwanted quantity.” That is the most trivial rational form imaginable. Claiming identity because they share the fraction bar is like calling circumference, electrical power, and economic growth “the same equation” because they each contain a product of a constant and a squared term (2πr, VI, r²). Any two-variable ratio that subtracts a baseline in the numerator inevitably looks alike. The number of contexts where such a ratio arises by dimensional necessity approaches ubiquity; thus, the appearance of the fraction bar is not evidence of an ontological link.

5. Category mistake between metaphor and isomorphism  
Physics equations instantiate real quantities measured with instruments; theological terms are mental or cultural constructs. Mapping them 1-to-1 commits a category error analogous to equating the wavefunction’s complex phase with “hope.” An isomorphism requires structure-preserving bijection; the mapping here is metaphorical at best. No experiment can vary “grace” as an independent variable and measure curvature of spacetime in response; hence no joint manifold exists. Absent cross-domain operationalisation, the claim of isomorphism dissolves.

6. Statistical sleight of hand  
The 10⁻¹⁰⁰ probability quote assumes independence among the 100 mappings, but the mappings share authorship, sources, and language; they are maximally dependent. The calculation is therefore meaningless—a quantitative smokescreen exploiting innumeracy.

Hardest-to-rebut point  
Point 3 (vocabulary dilution) is the gravest threat. If the key terms have no domain-independent operational definition that produces different numerical answers for competing theories, then T is not falsifiable and collapses into a rhetoric system, not a scientific one.
