MATH TRANSLATION LAYER (MTL) - Theophysics
Equation Presentation Template v2.0 · POF 2828

You are given one document. Find EVERY equation in it and render each one in the
six-part format below. Nothing else. No preamble, no summary, no commentary.

If the document contains no equations, output exactly: NO EQUATIONS FOUND

============================================================
FOR EACH EQUATION, OUTPUT EXACTLY THIS STRUCTURE:
============================================================

## EQ-<nn> - <the equation's name>

**ANCHOR**

One or two sentences. Who wrote this physics, when, and what they were actually
working on at the time. Then the turn: the same equation, unchanged, describes
the spiritual structure. If the source does not identify an originating
physicist, write the anchor from the physics itself and say so.

**THE EQUATION**

$$<clean LaTeX>$$

**WORD FORM**

Rewrite the equation in words, in the SAME SHAPE as the symbols. Every symbol
becomes its plain-English name; every operator stays where it is. Example:

    what's free to act = total energy minus grace intensity x accumulated disorder

This is the most important line in the whole output. A reader who cannot read
mathematics must be able to read the equation here.

**TERMS**

One row per symbol. Two meanings side by side.

| Term | PHYSICS | SPIRITUAL |
|---|---|---|
| <symbol> | <what it means in the physics> | <what it means in the spiritual reading> |

If the source gives only one side, fill that side and write "not stated in
source" in the other. Never invent the missing side.

**IN PLAIN ENGLISH**

One paragraph. No symbols, no jargon. Say what the equation asserts, then say
what happens at the boundaries - what it means when a term goes to zero, and
what it means when a term is large. The boundaries are where the meaning lives.

**STRUCTURAL INSIGHT**

Pick the heading that fits and use it verbatim:

- "WHY THE STRUCTURE MATTERS" - when the equation's form (multiplicative,
  exponential, logarithmic, differential) carries the argument
- "WHAT THIS EQUATION PROVES" - when specific consequences follow; number them
- "WHY THIS IS NOT ANALOGY" - only when the physics and spiritual equations
  share functional form AND boundary behavior

Then the content: what the shape of the math forces to be true. Not what it
suggests - what it forces.

**BRIDGE GRADE**

Exactly one word, then one sentence of justification:

- STRUCTURAL_IDENTITY - same equation, same variables, only labels differ
- STRUCTURAL_ISOMORPHISM - different equations, same functional form and same
  boundary behavior
- STRUCTURAL_ANALOGY - similar shape, but does not constrain predictions in
  both directions
- METAPHORICAL - evocative resemblance only
- NOT_A_BRIDGE - the equation makes no cross-domain claim

**FLAGS**

List any that apply, or write "none":
- UNDEFINED_TERM: a symbol appears with no definition anywhere in the source
- DIMENSIONAL_MISMATCH: terms added or equated do not share units
- FREE_PARAMETER: a constant or exponent is posited rather than derived
- OVERSTATED: prose around the equation claims more than the equation shows

============================================================
DUAL-DOMAIN VARIANT
============================================================

When the source presents a PHYSICS equation and a SPIRITUAL equation as two
forms of the same structure, show both under THE EQUATION, stacked:

    PHYSICS    $$C = B \log_2(1 + S/N)$$
       <->
    SPIRITUAL  $$C = A \log_2(1 + T/D)$$

Then the TERMS table maps them term to term with <-> between the columns, and
the STRUCTURAL INSIGHT heading is "WHY THIS IS NOT ANALOGY".

============================================================
RULES
============================================================

1. Report what the source says. Do not repair, extend, or improve its math.
2. If a symbol is never defined, say so in FLAGS. Do not guess its meaning.
3. Never upgrade a bridge grade to make the source look stronger. When the
   evidence sits between two grades, choose the weaker one.
4. The WORD FORM and IN PLAIN ENGLISH sections must contain no symbols at all.
5. Preserve the source's own equation IDs; put them in parentheses after EQ-nn.
6. Output raw markdown. No code fences around the whole response.
